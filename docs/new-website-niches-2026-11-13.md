# New Website Niches: Keyword Research Summary (13 Nov 2026)

All volumes are estimated ranges from free sources (SEMrush/SimilarWeb public pages, Ahrefs data quoted publicly, Google Ads data quoted in blogs, Clicks.so, Treendly). Different tools disagree by 2 to 3x. Nothing here is a guaranteed figure.

## Rank 1: Pakistan Results + Jobs Hub

Proven demand in Pakistan. Incumbent scale: ilmkidunya roughly 1 to 3M visits per month, rozee.pk about 3.3M visits per month.

Top keywords (estimates):
- jobs in pakistan / latest jobs in pakistan: 500K to 1.5M per month aggregate, steady all year, high competition (Rozee, PaperPK)
- [board] 10th class result, per big board: 100K to 500K in result month, near zero off season, medium competition
- govt jobs in pakistan (PPSC, FPSC, Pak Army, Punjab Police variants): 50K to 300K per major ad, medium competition
- date sheet per board: 50K to 200K in season, low to medium competition
- scholarships (HEC, Ehsaas, fully funded): 100K to 400K aggregate, low to medium competition
- admissions + merit lists: 200K to 600K aggregate in season, medium competition
- India side (optional, biggest volume, hardest): sarkari result family, leader site gets tens of millions of visits per month, competition extreme

Concept: one fast site, all boards result pages (roll number and name search), date sheets, daily govt job posts from newspapers as clean text, scholarships and merit lists to fill the off season. Money: AdSense, WhatsApp/Telegram alert channels, test prep affiliates.
Risks: daily freshness grind, result day spikes need strong hosting, data comes from official sources, results traffic is seasonal (about 70 percent in 8 weeks).

## Rank 2: File Tools + Calculators (one tools domain)

Evergreen, near zero running cost, everything runs in the browser.

Top keywords (estimates):
- percentage calculator: 1.5M to 2.2M global, KD about 22, stable
- word counter: 850K to 1.65M global, KD about 22, stable
- age calculator: about 1.1M global, KD about 18, one of the softest big keywords
- merge pdf: about 1.7M in India alone, high competition at head, softer long tails
- compress pdf: 1.3M to 1.6M in India alone; exact size variants like compress pdf to 200kb are the winnable layer
- image compressor + exact KB variants: about 490K global, medium competition
- qr code generator: about 673K per month, rising slowly, high competition
- emi calculator: 200K+ in India, medium competition
- mortgage calculator: 500K to 1.5M global, US heavy, highest money (CPC 15 to 50 dollars)
- bmi calculator: 600K to 1.5M global, medium to high competition
- password generator: 165K to 450K, low competition on variants
- typing test: 500K to 1M, rising

Concept: exact size file tools (compress PDF/image to exact KB targets, merge, split, image to PDF) plus a finance and everyday calculator hub (percentage, EMI, mortgage, age, BMI, GPA). Money: AdSense, finance affiliates, later a Pro tier.
Risks: head terms take 6 to 12 months, PK/IN traffic pays low RPM (1 to 4 dollars), so US/EU calculator traffic matters. Google answer boxes eat simple converter queries, interactive tools are safer.

## Rank 3: Cricket Records + PSL Hub

Fits the existing cricket audience. Data: Cricsheet, free ball by ball data for 23,241 matches, commercial use allowed with attribution.

Top keywords (estimates):
- live cricket score: 11M to 13.6M in India, very high competition, do NOT target (rights + Cricbuzz wall)
- psl (Pakistan): 4.09M; psl 2026 schedule: 1.22M, medium competition, best Pakistan opening
- ipl points table family: 673K to 5M by variant, high but beatable
- t20 world cup schedule: 7.48M in India during event years
- icc rankings: about 823K, medium to high competition
- virat kohli stats: 100K to 400K, medium competition, small dated sites rank
- records family (most runs in odi, fastest century, etc.): small each, thousands of variants, evergreen, low to medium competition, the winnable layer
- india vs pakistan head to head: tiny long tails, huge match day spikes, low to medium competition

Concept: interactive records and stats database (filters by format, team, venue, era), player pages, head to head explorer, plus PSL/IPL schedule and points table section for seasonal spikes. Money: AdSense only.
Risks: live scores cannot be scraped (licence needed), seasonality about 5x peak to trough, fantasy/betting promotion is illegal in India and Pakistan, avoid completely.

## Rank 4: AI Detector + AI Utilities

Biggest raw volumes, hardest fight.
- ai detector: about 4.5M to 5M US per month, high competition (ZeroGPT gets 20M+ visits)
- ai image generator: 1M to 2.7M global, high competition
- background remover: about 823K US, valuable CPC about 4 dollars, high organic competition
- ai humanizer: 250K to 1M US, seasonal with school calendar, KD about 69
- ai video generator: about 470K global, rising fast, expensive to serve
- text to speech: about 301K US, medium competition, evergreen
- breakout micro terms: ai music detector +1600 percent in 2 years, ai voice detector, low competition now

Concept: multi format detector hub (text, image, voice, music) or a free AI utilities suite. Money: freemium + ads.
Risks: running cost per use, detection accuracy arms race, Google and chat apps absorb features, seasonality. High risk, high cost.

## Rank 5: Templates + Generators

- cover letter template: 1.0M to 1.3M global, high competition
- resume template: 800K to 1.2M global, high competition, long tails per job title are the entry
- font generator: 600K to 800K, medium to high competition, cheap to build
- invoice generator/template: 50K to 300K, highest CPCs (4 to 17 dollars), business users, best money per visitor
- username generator: about 160K global, medium competition, stable
- name generators (niche variants): KD as low as 7 to 15, the classic winnable pattern
- Wallpaper/font download hubs: huge site level traffic (DaFont 16M to 26M visits) but serious copyright risk, not recommended

Concept: small business document hub (invoice generator + templates + name generators) for money, or creator text tools (font/username/bio generators) as a cheap traffic engine.

## Recommendation

1. Results + Jobs hub: fastest realistic path to big Pakistan traffic, medium competition, programmatic playbook fits.
2. Tools domain: best evergreen asset, near zero cost, slower ramp, balances income with US/EU traffic.
3. Cricket records + PSL: best fit with existing audience, good second site or phase 2.

Suggested order: build one first, properly. Results hub if speed matters most, tools domain if a long term evergreen asset matters most.
